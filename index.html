How this is calculated

  • Savings: interest is added every month at the yearly rate ÷ 12. The final amount is your monthly contribution compounded over every month of the period, with contributions made at the end of each month.
  • Loan: repayments are equal each month on a reducing balance. Each month's interest is charged only on what you still owe, so interest falls and principal rises over time.
  • Rates are entered per year. If your SACCO quotes a monthly rate (for example 1%), multiply by 12 first.

These are estimates. Your SACCO's processing fees, insurance and dividend rules are not included.
